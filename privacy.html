Privacy Policy for Sanctuary

Effective Date: September 5, 2026 · Developer: Aastik Tripathi

Sanctuary is built as a private, offline-first digital space for reflection, mood tracking, and mindful journaling. Your privacy and autonomy are core design principles of the application. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when using Sanctuary.

1. Summary: Zero Data Collection

Sanctuary does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or store any personal data on external servers, cloud databases, or third-party platforms. The app operates 100% on-device without requiring an internet connection or user account.

2. On-Device Data Storage

All data generated while using Sanctuary — including daily reflection notes, mood ratings, habit completions, symptom records, and analytical logs — is saved strictly in your device's private local application sandbox.

This data never leaves your physical phone unless you explicitly choose to export a backup file via the app's export tool.

3. Local Machine Learning & Analytics

Any insights, habit correlations, or symptom trend analyses generated by Sanctuary are computed entirely on your device using local linear regression and on-device natural language models. No queries, text snippets, or analytical vectors are ever transmitted to any remote API, server, or cloud service.

4. Device Permissions

Sanctuary requests only the minimal permissions required to provide its offline features:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to trigger gentle, scheduled daily reflection reminders configured on your device using Android's native alarm scheduler. No notification content or interaction data is tracked.

5. Third-Party Services and Telemetry

Sanctuary contains:

  • No advertisements or advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics tracking (no Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or third-party telemetry).
  • No social logins or third-party authentication trackers.

6. Data Retention, Backup, and Deletion

You retain complete ownership and control of your data:

  • Export & Backup: You can create a local JSON backup of your data at any time from the app settings to store wherever you choose.
  • Deletion: Uninstalling Sanctuary or clearing the app storage in your Android device settings immediately and permanently deletes all data stored by the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Sanctuary is intended for a general audience (aged 18 and older). Because the app collects no personal information whatsoever, it does not collect, use, or disclose personal data from children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

If changes are made to this policy in future versions of the application, the updated document will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
