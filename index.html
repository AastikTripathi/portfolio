Privacy-First On-Device ML Flutter & Dart

🌱 Sanctuary

Sanctuary is a premium, offline personal health journal designed for individuals managing chronic conditions and daily routines. It analyzes logs completely on-device using a custom local regression engine to surface patterns without compromising privacy.

L2 Regularized Ridge Regression

Isolates routine and symptom variables on-device to compute correlation impacts without cloud lag or telemetry.

Zero-Server Privacy Model

All tracking logs, NLP categorization tokens, and backups stay locally on-device in secure plaintext JSON files.

Streak & Interval Analytics

Calculates days elapsed since last flare-up, average symptom intervals, and longest symptom-free streaks.
